Method

32 Japanese sentences, three male voices, two speeds. Kokoro uses native synthesis speed; Qwen uses pitch-preserving atempo processing. Voice labels are hidden by default; the order is fixed for repeatable review. No ASR scores prefill human judgments. Deliberately incorrect control clips are excluded.
